About the game

Choose the shot. Open the breach.

Choose a cannonball, aim, charge the powder and fire at the rival fort. In V2, follow the campaign, build and test your own fort, prepare equipment in the armoury and preview cosmetic appearances in your profile. Local two-player duels take place on the same phone.
