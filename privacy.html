App privacy

Clear choices. Limited data.

This policy explains what stays on your device, what Google may process for optional rewarded ads, and what we receive only when you contact us.

Updated 31 July 2026

In short

Game progress and settings are stored locally.

Ads are optional and only reward a player after completion.

privacy@siegewinds.com

Scope This policy covers the Siege Winds mobile app and this static website. Advertising details apply only to builds in which rewarded ads are enabled.

01

Data kept on your device

Siege Winds stores progress and preferences locally so the game can work. This can include unlocks, campaign lives and checkpoints, in-game balances, cosmetic choices, match state, records, language, audio and vibration settings, and a randomly generated local guest identifier.

  • You do not need to provide a real name or email address for local play.
  • Siege Winds does not receive this local progress in the current ad-enabled build.
  • Your operating system may include app data in a device backup under your Apple or Google settings.

02

Optional rewarded ads

Siege Winds uses Google Mobile Ads only when you deliberately choose one of these rewards:

  • one campaign life; or
  • 10 purple gems for cosmetics, limited to three rewards per UTC day; or
  • a second deterministic fragment exchange that day, after the free exchange: spend 3 surplus cannon fragments to receive 1 fragment for the cannon you choose.

The reward is granted only after Google confirms completion. Purple gems unlock cosmetic items and do not add competitive power. Fragment exchanges never change fragment odds or cannon damage. With an active no-ads entitlement, Solo lives are unlimited, the purple-gem ad is not shown, and 30 purple gems are credited automatically once per UTC day instead. The entitlement does not grant the second fragment exchange. The app does not use banner, interstitial, native, or app-open ads.

03

Google consent and ad data

Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) runs before any ad request. Where consent is required, Siege Winds waits for a valid choice. If consent cannot be established, the app does not request an ad.

When an ad is requested, Google may process consent status, app and device information, IP-based approximate location, advertising identifiers when available, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and protect the ad service. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Siege Winds does not add an Apple App Tracking Transparency prompt. This does not mean Google processes no data. When Google requires privacy options, you can reopen them from Settings → Privacy & Support → Ad Privacy Choices.

04

Purchases, support, and this website

If an in-app purchase is available in your build, Apple or Google processes the payment. Siege Winds may receive a transaction reference and the resulting entitlement or cosmetic-currency grant, but not your full payment-card details.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachment you choose to send. We use it to answer the request and investigate the reported issue. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

This website adds no analytics, ads, tracking pixels, forms, or scripts and sets no cookies. GitHub Pages may process standard connection data to deliver and secure the pages under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

05

Your controls and contacts

You can remove local game data by using your device’s app-management controls or uninstalling the app. This can erase local progress. Google, Apple, the email provider, and the website host apply their own retention and privacy controls to data they process.

  • Privacy questions or requests: privacy@siegewinds.com
  • Player support: support@siegewinds.com

We make no fixed response-time promise. This policy will be updated if the app’s data use or services change.
